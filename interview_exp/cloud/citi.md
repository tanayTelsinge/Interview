## Citi's Technology Transformation — Current (2025-2026)

This is critical to know — Citi is in the middle of a massive transformation.

### Scale of Investment
- Spent **$11.8 billion on technology in 2024** alone
- Additional **$2.9 billion** in transformation initiatives
- Over **$30 billion invested in technology over last 3 years**
- Moving to **Google Cloud** for significant portions of financial infrastructure

### Why the Urgency
- 2020 Federal Reserve consent order — poor data quality management
- **$135.6 million in regulatory fines** for data quality deficits
- CEO Jane Fraser acknowledged "decades of historic underinvestment"
- Regulatory pressure driving urgency to modernize

### AI Push
- Armed **30,000 developers with Gen AI coding tools** (Jan 2025)
- Every executive leadership member tasked to identify **top 5 areas where AI can deliver impact**
- Active hiring for GenAI developers in Pune
- Citi Ventures investing in AI/ML startups

### Workforce Strategy
- Reducing reliance on external IT contractors by **20-50%**
- Increasing full-time technology workforce to **50,000**
- India set to become **second-largest Citi workforce globally**
- GCCs evolving from cost centers to **strategic innovation hubs**
